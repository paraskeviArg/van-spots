Heuristic scoring only. Signals: van-drivable road access, terrain slope from Open-Meteo elevation, coastal proximity, distance from buildings (discretion), water-source proximity, and hazard tags. Environmental flags shown for beaches (Peloponnese has loggerhead turtle nesting, especially Kyparissiakos Gulf and Lakonikos Bay — never park on sand), wetlands, cliff edges, and OSM-tagged protected areas. Cannot see fences, private-land boundaries not tagged in OSM, road width, or turnaround space. Combine with community-sourced apps for reviewed spots.
